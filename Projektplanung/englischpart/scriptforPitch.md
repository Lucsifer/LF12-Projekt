# Pitch Script
## Speaker 1 – Personalized Opening (30–45 seconds)
“Thank you for taking the time to meet with us today. We understand that your corporation is constantly exploring innovative IT solutions that are both practical and future‑oriented. With millions of people interacting digitally every day, reliable data access and user‑friendly interfaces are becoming more important than ever. That’s why we developed our League of Legends Summoner Stats Webapp — a modern, secure, and efficient platform that demonstrates how real‑time data, clean architecture, and scalable infrastructure can come together to create a seamless user experience. It’s a compact but powerful example of how we approach IT solutions: structured, transparent, and built for performance.”

## Speaker 2 – Addressing Needs & Pain Points (30–45 seconds)
“Many companies today struggle with fragmented data sources, slow response times, and systems that don’t scale well under high user load. In gaming environments like League of Legends, these challenges become even more visible: users expect instant access to accurate stats, smooth performance, and zero downtime. These expectations mirror the challenges large enterprises face in their own IT landscapes. Our project tackles exactly these issues by integrating external APIs, handling rate limits, and ensuring that data is delivered quickly and reliably — all while keeping the system simple to maintain and easy to extend.”

## Speaker 3 – Data‑Driven Solution & Technical Value (30–45 seconds)
“Our solution combines a modern backend with the official Riot Games API, a Redis caching layer for high‑speed data delivery, and a secure server infrastructure managed through Docker and Nginx. This setup reduces API calls, improves performance, and ensures that frequently accessed data is available instantly. On top of that, we implemented monitoring with Uptime Kuma to track system health and uptime in real time. The result is a stable, scalable, and secure application that demonstrates how even complex data environments can be handled efficiently. In similar setups, companies have seen up to 40% faster response times and significantly reduced server load thanks to caching and containerization.”

## Speaker 4 – Confirmation & Clear Next Steps (30–45 seconds)
“What makes this project especially valuable is that it’s not just a gaming tool — it’s a blueprint for modern IT architecture. The same principles we used here can be applied to business dashboards, customer portals, analytics platforms, or any system that relies on fast, reliable data access. We would be happy to walk you through a live demo to show how the app works, how the infrastructure is structured, and how these concepts can be adapted to your organization’s needs. If you’re interested, we can schedule a follow‑up meeting to explore potential use cases and integration possibilities.”
